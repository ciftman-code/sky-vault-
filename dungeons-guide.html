CATACOMBS FIELD GUIDE · PARTY PREPARATION

Enter the Catacombs with a plan

Dungeons reward preparation, communication and repeatable execution. This guide helps you choose a role, prepare a sensible loadout and improve each run without treating gear price as the only measure of readiness.

Read the field guide Open Item Vault

🏰 A steady Catacombs route

  1. Learn the room flow. Understand how exploration, clearing, secrets, puzzles and the boss phase fit together before rushing for completion time.
  2. Choose a class you can support. Berserk, Mage, Archer, Tank and Healer have different jobs. Pick a role that fits your gear and what your party needs.
  3. Practice on a floor you can complete consistently. Stable clears teach room layouts and boss patterns better than repeatedly joining runs that are far beyond your current setup.
  4. Improve one weakness at a time. Work on survival, damage, secrets, class ability timing or route knowledge instead of buying several upgrades without knowing what is holding you back.
  5. Raise your Catacombs progression gradually. Check floor requirements and gear restrictions in-game; they can change and some items have specific dungeon conditions.
Best improvement habit

After a run, identify one thing that slowed the party down and practice that in the next run. This is more useful than changing your entire loadout after every mistake.

🧩 Understand each class

⚔️ Berserk

Close-range damage and staying alive while fighting near enemies. Positioning and defensive choices matter when rooms become crowded.

🏹 Archer

Ranged damage and target priority. Keep line of sight, manage distance and coordinate on high-priority targets.

🔮 Mage

Ability-driven damage and room clearing. Learn ability timing, mana management and how your weapon interacts with the dungeon setup.

🛡️ Tank

Party protection and enemy control. Staying alive and understanding where to position can make a run more consistent.

💚 Healer

Support and recovery. Watch party health, understand your tools and avoid separating so far that support cannot reach teammates.

🤝 Team flow

Agree on roles, communicate puzzle needs, avoid duplicate effort and regroup before the boss phase when needed.

Secrets and room knowledge

Secrets are not just a completion checkbox. Learning common room layouts and the tools needed for each secret improves consistency. Use a trusted current route reference, but still learn the room itself so a small variation does not stop your run.

🚧 Common run-killers

  • Joining a floor without meeting its requirements or understanding its boss mechanics.
  • Using a class setup that does not match the party role you selected.
  • Ignoring secrets, puzzles or room progression and expecting the team to compensate.
  • Spending all coins on damage while leaving survivability too low.
  • Leaving the party without communicating, or repeatedly restarting without learning what failed.

✅ Pre-run checklist

  • Check the floor and party requirements.
  • Confirm your selected class and role.
  • Equip the correct dungeon loadout and utility items.
  • Know the boss mechanics you are about to face.
  • Have a plan for secrets, puzzles and communication.
  • After the run, note one improvement for next time.

Frequently asked questions

Which class should a new player choose?

Choose the role you understand and can equip reliably. Learn one class first, then expand when you understand how parties balance damage, support and survival.

Should I buy the most expensive dungeon weapon?

Not automatically. Verify floor access, class use, dungeon requirements and the weakness it will solve. A balanced setup and better room knowledge can be more valuable.

Why do parties care about secrets?

Secrets contribute to dungeon score and reflect room knowledge. Learn common layouts and coordinate with teammates so rooms are completed consistently.

🗺️ Room flow, secrets and score

A Catacombs run has two jobs happening at once: complete the dungeon rooms and prepare the party for the boss. Good runs come from clear communication and knowing what each room still needs.

Clear rooms methodically

Check the room state, identify unfinished mobs or objectives and communicate when a room is complete. Avoid leaving unclear sections for the next teammate.

Secrets and exploration

Learn common secret locations and the interaction required to reach them. A player who knows the map can help the group finish consistently without rushing past objectives.

Puzzles

Read the puzzle room and coordinate one person at a time when the task requires sequence or shared attention. If a room is unfamiliar, pause and work it out instead of guessing repeatedly.

Boss transition

Before entering the boss phase, check the party's readiness and confirm that everyone understands their role. Do not assume another player has completed your room tasks.

After-run review

Note the room or objective that cost the party the most time. Learn that one section before changing your whole setup or moving to a harder floor.
