GARDEN PROGRESSION · PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

Greenhouse & Garden Guide

A structured route for learning the Garden systems and organizing your farming progress. Hypixel can change requirements and rewards, so use the in-game Garden menu and current official wiki for exact values.

Progression route

1
Unlock and explore the Garden

Complete the introductory tasks, meet the Garden NPCs and learn where the plot, desk and visitor systems are located.

2
Open plots gradually

Use the Garden's plot menu to see each unlock requirement. Focus on reachable milestones instead of spending resources on every plot at once.

3
Build a reliable crop setup

Choose a crop, make sure the farm layout is comfortable to use, and check that your tool and equipment match the crop.

4
Work through crop milestones

Check crop milestones and collection goals in-game. Rotate crops when a milestone or visitor request gives you a useful reason.

5
Learn visitors and commissions

Review visitor requests before accepting them, and keep track of the materials required so you can plan farming sessions.

6
Improve farming equipment

Compare farming tools, armor, reforges and pets for your current crop and budget. Use the item database and calculators to compare costs.

7
Understand pests and upgrades

Use the current in-game Garden menu to learn pest mechanics, available upgrades and any greenhouse-related features present in your game version.
