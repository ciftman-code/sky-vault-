The Garden: from first plot to a purpose-built farm

The Garden is more than planting rows and selling crops. Your progress connects plots, crop milestones, visitors, Composter upgrades, Farming Fortune, collections and the equipment you choose for each crop.

🌱 1. A useful Garden progression route

  1. Finish Sam's introduction. Follow the Garden questline and learn the plot menu, Garden Desk, visitor area and the Composter unlock. Do not skip the NPC instructions; the introduction teaches the core loop.
  2. Unlock plots with a reason. Check each plot's requirement from the Desk. Pick the next plot that supports your current crop or milestone rather than spending every resource the moment it becomes available.
  3. Choose a crop objective. Decide whether this session is for a collection milestone, a visitor request, a contest target, Composter input or coins. The reason changes which crop is useful.
  4. Match your setup to the crop. Check the tool, armor, equipment, pet and relevant farming stats. A farming setup should be compared by its useful stats and requirements, not by the item's name or rarity alone.
  5. Keep visitors and upgrades moving. Review visitor offers, copper and Composter needs, then group the items you must gather into one or two focused farming sessions.
Farming rule

Do not treat every crop as a pure coin farm. A crop can be valuable to your account because it advances a milestone, satisfies a visitor, supplies the Composter or contributes to a recipe.

🧭 2. Plots, Garden Desk and crop milestones

The Garden Desk is the place to review plot unlocks and crop progression. Use it as a checklist rather than guessing which plot or crop should come next.

Plot unlocks

Open the Desk and read the requirement displayed for the next plot. Keep a note of any crop, collection or Garden task you still need, then work on one requirement at a time.

Crop milestones

Milestones are crop-specific progress targets. Check the milestone panel before choosing a farm so that the crop you harvest advances a goal you actually care about.

Garden visitors

Visitors can request selected farming materials. Their offers may include rewards such as copper, Garden experience or other progression rewards. Compare the requested items with the reward before accepting.

Composter upgrades

Compost is a Garden resource used by Garden progression. Upgrades affect production speed, resource limits, extra output chances or the inputs required.

Make the next unlock visible

Write down the exact item or crop requirement shown by the game. If you are short on a resource, check its SkyBlock ID in the Item Vault and compare current market values in the Bazaar Explorer before deciding whether to gather or buy it.

📦 4. Garden visitors: requests, rewards and preparation

Visitor requests are one of the main ways the Garden links your crop supply to Garden progression. A request is not automatically worth completing just because it is available.

  1. Read the complete offer and list every requested crop or item.
  2. Check the displayed reward and whether it advances a milestone you currently want.
  3. Estimate the gathering time or purchase cost for missing items.
  4. Keep common visitor items in a local shopping list so you can prepare several requests in one trip.
  5. Accept only after you understand the request and are ready to provide the required items.

Use the visitor shopping list on this page as a planning note. It does not connect to Hypixel or automatically read visitor offers from your account.

♻️ 5. The Composter: Organic Matter, Fuel and Compost

The Composter converts crop inputs into Organic Matter and combines that with Fuel to produce Compost. You unlock access through Sam's initial Garden questline, then use the Composter menu to inspect available storage, production and upgrades.

Organic Matter

Different crops and enchanted crops contribute different amounts. Check the item tooltip or current Composter menu before feeding it valuable materials.

Fuel

Fuel is the second production input. Keep an eye on both meters; having only one input stocked will not keep production moving.

Compost

Compost is the output used for Garden progression. Plan its production around the upgrades or unlocks that actually need it.

Upgrade choices

Composter upgrades can affect production speed, input caps, input costs and the chance of extra Compost. Compare the displayed cost with the benefit to your routine.

Useful baseline: the public Wiki currently lists a default recipe of 4,000 Organic Matter plus 2,000 Fuel for one Compost, with a 10-minute production time. Treat this as a reference only and check the live in-game Composter menu because game values may change.

Reference: Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki — Composter.

🐛 6. Pests, Farming Contests and crop variety

Pests are a Garden system that can interrupt or change the rhythm of farming. Learn how the current pest appears, which Garden menu or tool handles it and what the reward is before spending time chasing it. Pest details and reward values can change, so use the current in-game interface as the source of truth.

Farming Contests

Jacob's Farming Contest gives you a reason to focus on a particular crop for a timed session. Prepare the correct crop area and tool, check the contest crop and schedule in-game, and compare your result after the contest rather than assuming one setup wins every crop.

When to rotate crops

  • A crop milestone or collection is the next useful account unlock.
  • A visitor requests a crop you do not have ready.
  • A contest gives you a reason to focus on a different crop.
  • You need a particular crop as Organic Matter or for a crafting recipe.
  • You are testing which crop setup works best with your current equipment.

Frequently asked questions

Should I farm only the crop with the highest Bazaar price?

No. Collection progress, visitors, Composter needs, contest goals and crop-specific upgrades can be more useful than the highest displayed price.

Does Farming Fortune work the same way as Farming Speed?

No. They are different stats. Check the in-game stat description and test the crop setup you are using; do not treat movement speed as a substitute for crop bonus stats.

Can I use the Composter before the Garden introduction?

The current Wiki states that the Composter is unlocked as you progress through Sam's initial questline. Follow the in-game quest prompts to unlock it.

Does this page read my Garden account data?

No. The guide and checklist are local planning tools. It does not connect to your Hypixel account or collect your inventory.
