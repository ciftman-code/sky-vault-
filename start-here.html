🧭 What to progress first on a SkyBlock profile

Hypixel SkyBlock is built around several account systems. A strong early plan does not mean buying one expensive item; it means opening systems that make many future activities easier.

SkyBlock Levels

Open your SkyBlock Level menu and review the available tasks. Levels are a profile-wide progress signal, not a direct measure of how much money you have.

Skills & Collections

Skills and collections unlock recipes, rewards and future goals. Check the next collection milestone before selling every resource you gather.

Minions

Minions generate resources on your island. Upgrade them when the collection unlock, storage or action speed is useful to your account, and account for fuel.

Accessories & Magical Power

Accessories go into the Accessory Bag. Maxwell at the Thaumaturgist lets you choose a Power and tune stats; the best Power depends on the stats your build needs.

Museum

Museum donations connect items to account progression and game milestones. Read the donation value and any restrictions before giving away something you still need.

Garden & Mining

These have their own progression trees and unlocks. Choose one specialty after learning the basics instead of trying to max every system at once.

The 18 November follow-up added a menu button to choose whether the Personal Compactor respects the 25% threshold rule.

💡 Before spending coins on an upgrade

  1. Identify the activity you want to improve.
  2. Find the exact problem: a missing requirement, a weak stat, a collection unlock or a quality-of-life issue.
  3. Check the item's SkyBlock ID and in-game requirements using the Item Vault.
  4. Compare current market data in the Bazaar Explorer and recipe ingredients in Craft Flip.
  5. Keep enough coins for the next progression requirement rather than spending your entire balance on one purchase.
Important: game balance and item values change. This is a planning guide, not a fixed meta or guarantee that one item is best for every player.
