Privacy Policy

SkyBlock Hub is an independent, unofficial fan-made companion for Hypixel SkyBlock. This page explains the information that may be processed when you use the site.

Information you provide

You can browse the item database, guides and calculators without creating an account. If you use the player lookup, the Minecraft Java username you enter is sent to our server to request publicly available SkyBlock profile information. Do not submit passwords, Microsoft login details, API keys or other secrets.

Local browser storage

Some optional checklist and planner features save your progress in your browser's local storage on your device. This information is not an account and may be cleared through your browser settings.

Server and service information

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most hosted websites, the hosting provider may process technical request information (such as IP address, browser details, timestamps and requested paths) for delivery, security and troubleshooting under its own policies. Requests for game data may also be sent to third-party services, including Hypixel or public game-data providers, as needed for the feature you choose.

Advertising and analytics

At the time of this update, SkyBlock Hub does not intentionally load Google AdSense, another advertising network, or a third-party analytics tag. No ads are currently being served by this site. If advertising or analytics is enabled in the future, this policy and any required user notices will be updated to describe the providers and their data practices.

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to serve, measure and help prevent invalid activity involving ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to show ads based on a visitor's previous visits to this site or other websites. The use of personalized advertising can be managed through Google Ads Settings. Visitors can also learn about options for some third-party vendors at YourAdChoices. These disclosures describe possible future advertising; they do not mean that advertising is currently active on SkyBlock Hub.

Cookies and choices

The site does not currently require an account or intentionally use advertising cookies. Hosting or embedded third-party services may have their own technical storage practices. If advertising is introduced, ad-related cookies and similar technologies may be used as described above. You can use browser controls to manage local storage and cookies, although blocking some storage may affect site features.

Children and families

The site is a general gaming resource. Please avoid submitting personal details. Parents or guardians should review third-party services and advertising settings where relevant. Advertising features, if introduced, will be configured in accordance with applicable Google policies and laws.

External websites

Links to Hypixel, Mojang/Microsoft, community tools or other websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are controlled by those services, not by SkyBlock Hub.

Changes and contact

This policy may be revised as site features change. For privacy questions or corrections, use the project's GitHub issue tracker; do not post private information or credentials in a public issue.
