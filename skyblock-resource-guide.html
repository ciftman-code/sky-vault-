Mining materials

Mining progression uses several resource families. Keep track of what an item unlocks, where it comes from, and whether you need it for a collection, an upgrade, or a crafting recipe. Do not judge a resource only by its current market price: collection progress and upgrade requirements can make it useful even when its sale value is low.

Mithril and Titanium

Track collection milestones and the materials required by the next upgrade. Check the current item entry before planning a long gathering session.

Gemstone resources

Compare the value of the raw resource with any crafted form, and include the cost of extra materials in your estimate.

Use the item database to look up an item's ID and published category, then compare its current market information in the Bazaar explorer.

Farming resources

Farming is easier to plan when you separate crop collection, Garden progression, and selling decisions. A crop can be useful for collections or crafting even when its immediate sale price is not the highest.

  • Check which crop or collection milestone you are working toward.
  • Compare selling the raw crop with selling an enchanted or crafted form.
  • Include the materials consumed by compaction or crafting in the cost.
  • Review Garden upgrades and plot goals before changing your whole routine.

Continue with the Garden guide for a structured farming overview.

Minion planning

A minion's result depends on more than its tier. Action speed, fuel, storage capacity, collection interval, and the way you sell the output all matter. A setup that looks strong for a short collection window may not be convenient when left running longer.

  • Set a realistic collection interval.
  • Account for fuel and upgrade costs.
  • Check whether storage will fill before you return.
  • Compare NPC value and market value instead of assuming one is always better.

Use the Minion Planner to organize your assumptions and estimate output.

How to research item prices

The Bazaar is a player-driven market, so prices and order depth can change. An estimate should distinguish between buying from sell offers and selling into buy orders. A displayed spread is not the same as guaranteed profit: fees, order competition, quantity, and fill time matter.

Good habit: check the current market, calculate the total material cost, and keep a margin for price movement. Treat calculators as estimates, not guarantees.

For recipes, compare the value of the finished item with the cost of every ingredient. Open Craft Flip for a structured calculation.

Quick research checklist

  1. Find the exact SkyBlock ID in the item database.
  2. Confirm its category and rarity.
  3. Check whether it is needed for a collection, recipe, or upgrade.
  4. Compare current market value with NPC value where available.
  5. Include every ingredient, fee, and collection cost in your estimate.
  6. Recheck the numbers before making a decision because game data can change.

Frequently asked questions

Does SkyBlock Hub need a Hypixel API key?

No personal key is needed for the public item catalog and public Bazaar data. Player-profile lookup is a separate optional feature.

Why do some item icons look different from the game?

The public item resource supplies a base Minecraft material. Some custom resource-pack textures or models are not represented by that basic icon.

Should I trust a calculator's profit estimate?

Use it as a starting point. Prices, order depth, fees, and material costs can change, so verify the live market before relying on an estimate.
