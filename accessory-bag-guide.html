The redesigned Accessory Bag

Hypixel's 0.27.3 announcement describes a new menu built around a distinct entry for each accessory. This guide explains the menu features, the summary widget and a sensible first review of your collection. It does not need a player API key.

What changed in the menu?

The old open-inventory layout has been replaced by a menu with a dedicated entry for each accessory. Hypixel says this makes the collection easier to inspect and manage.

Find gaps

Distinct entries make accessories you do not own easier to spot.

Search and sort

Search by name, or sort by name, rarity and game stage.

Check the route

View an accessory's obtainment method or upgrade method directly in-game.

Manage in place

Toggle or open accessory menus and upgrade accessories without first taking them out of the bag.

The update also adds an Accessory Bag to The Rift. Treat it as a separate menu to check when you are progressing in that area.

A practical first review

  1. Open the Accessory Bag. Start with the in-game menu rather than relying on an old screenshot or a pre-update checklist.
  2. Read the summary widget. Note your total Accessory Power, selected Power, Tuning Points and available bag slots.
  3. Sort by game stage. Use the built-in sort to scan the collection in a progression-friendly order; it is an organizing aid, not a personalized recommendation.
  4. Search for one accessory. Open its entry and read the obtainment or upgrade information before planning around it.
  5. Check the Rift separately. The official notes announce a Rift Accessory Bag as well.
Keep it simple: choose one missing accessory or one upgrade path to investigate at a time. The menu helps you find information; it does not mean every missing entry should be pursued immediately.

Understand the Accessory Bag widget

Hypixel says the new widget shows four pieces of information. These are labels for what the game displays, not values pulled from your account.

Accessory Power is now listed first in each accessory's stats. Enrichments now provide one Tuning Point instead of a specific stat. Review your selected Power and tuning in-game; this guide does not prescribe one setup for every activity.

Extra slots and in-game coin costs

  • Hypixel's notes say players receive nine additional Accessory Bag slots by default.
  • The notes say Jacobus slot upgrades now cap at 10 million in-game coins each, and the total cost to max the upgrades is about 870 million in-game coins, down from about 1.8 billion.
  • Hypixel also says earlier tiers are cheaper and that previous spending differences were refunded, so buying before or after the change should lead to the same total coin cost.
Before spending: these figures refer only to virtual SkyBlock coins in the official 0.27.3 notes. Check Jacobus's current in-game menu before making a purchase; do not treat an old guide as a live quote.

Related quality-of-life change: Personal Compactor

The Personal Compactor was changed in the same update, but it is separate from the Accessory Bag. It no longer uses item slots. Instead, you choose which items in your Sacks should be compacted automatically.

  • Each core skill, plus Husbandry, has its own Personal Compactor unlocked early in that skill's collection.
  • Compacting now happens directly in Sacks.
  • The update adds the /compactor and /deleter Cookie Buff commands for access to those menus.

Check the relevant collection and your in-game menu for the exact unlock requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see which accessories I am missing?

Yes. The distinct-entry layout is designed to make missing accessories easier to identify.

Can I search by name?

Yes. Hypixel's notes describe name search and sorting by name, rarity and game stage.

Does The Rift have its own Accessory Bag?

Yes. The official 0.27.3 notes announce an Accessory Bag for The Rift.

Do enrichments still grant a specific stat?

No. The official notes say they now grant one Tuning Point instead.
