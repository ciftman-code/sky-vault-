About SkyBlock Hub

SkyBlock Hub is an independent fan-made toolkit designed to make Hypixel SkyBlock information easier to explore.

What you can do

  • Search a growing item database and browse item guides.
  • Explore Bazaar, craft-flip and NPC price tools.
  • Plan minions, Garden goals and progression.
  • Use calculators and optional public player-profile lookup.

Our approach

Core tools should remain easy to access. If the project introduces sponsorships or advertising in the future, they should be clearly identified and kept separate from interactive game tools and search results.

SkyBlock Hub is not affiliated with Hypixel, Mojang or Microsoft.
