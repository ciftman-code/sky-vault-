How Bazaar flipping is estimated

The calculator compares the current average buy-order price with the current average sell-offer price reported by Hypixel. It subtracts the selected Bazaar sale tax from the sell side, then shows the estimated net difference and return on the buy price.

  • Buy order: estimated price paid when your buy order fills.
  • Sell offer: estimated listing price when your sell offer fills.
  • Net profit: sell offer after selected tax, minus buy price.
  • ROI: estimated net profit divided by buy price.

Use volume and budget filters

The 7-day traded-units figure uses the lower of Hypixel's buy-side and sell-side moving-week totals as a conservative volume reference. Order book depth shows the lower of currently listed buy and sell quantities. The budget filter estimates how many units fit within the chosen coin amount; it does not guarantee that all units can fill at the displayed price.
