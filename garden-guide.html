Hypixel SkyBlock Garden & Greenhouse Guide

A clear route from your first Garden levels to the Greenhouse, with the important differences between crop milestones, visitors, pests, mutations and the Crop Analyzer.

Game facts checked 13 November 2026. Skyblock Hub is an unofficial fan-made guide.

Quick route: unlock The Garden at SkyBlock Level 5 → earn Garden XP from crop milestones and visitors → reach Garden VII → complete the Carpenter's first visitor offer → give the Greenhouse Blueprint to Sam.

Garden plots and the Greenhouse are different

The regular Garden is where you build Garden levels by harvesting crops and serving visitors. Crop milestones are tied to harvesting on The Garden; harvesting elsewhere or relying on automated collection does not advance those milestones.

The Greenhouse is a separate Garden plot. Its crops can keep growing while you are offline, and the way you arrange nearby crops can create mutations. That makes it a planning system as well as a farming area.

Important: ordinary Garden crops do not keep growing when you leave the Garden. The offline-growth rule applies to Greenhouse crops, not every Garden plot.

Progress through The Garden

  1. Unlock The Garden. The Garden becomes available at SkyBlock Level 5. Use the Garden's Desk to check plots, crop milestones and upgrades.
  2. Earn Garden XP. Harvest crops on The Garden to advance their crop milestones. Accept visitor offers you can complete; visitor milestones also reward progression.
  3. Keep an eye on pests. Pests become part of the Garden loop at Garden Level 5. Clear them when they build up, because several pests can reduce your Farming Fortune.
  4. Work toward Garden VII. Continue crop milestones and visitor offers until you reach Garden Level 7. This is the level gate for the Greenhouse visitor path.
If a crop milestone seems stuck, check that you are harvesting that crop on The Garden yourself. Crops harvested on another island do not count for the Garden milestone.

Unlock the first Greenhouse

  1. Reach Garden Level 7.
  2. Wait for the Carpenter to appear as a Garden Visitor, then complete the offer.
  3. The first completed Carpenter offer grants the Greenhouse Blueprint.
  4. Give the Blueprint to Sam to unlock the Greenhouse, then use the Desk to choose the plot you want to convert.

The Blueprint is not a normal Bazaar purchase. If the Carpenter has not appeared yet, keep progressing the Garden and check the visitor area again.

Greenhouse basics: water, crop effects and mutations

Water your crops

Greenhouse crops lose water as they grow. If a crop reaches a negative water level during a growth stage, it may fail to advance. Check the crop's status and keep its water supply maintained.

Read the nearby effects

Crops and mutations can affect other planting slots next to them. The current community-wiki mechanics describe these effects as spreading to side-adjacent slots, not diagonal ones. Some effects help yield, experience or water retention; others can be drawbacks.

Learn mutation patterns gradually

Mutations can appear when the required crops are arranged in specific patterns around a suitable empty slot. Start by learning one pattern at a time and check the effect shown in-game before building a larger layout.

Patch-awareness: the community wiki marks its Greenhouse and Mutations pages as still being updated for patch 0.27.2. Treat older layout charts and exact yield numbers as provisional, and confirm the current in-game information before committing a large layout.

When does the Crop Analyzer unlock?

The Crop Analyzer is not automatically unlocked just because you have the Greenhouse. Continue Beth's later quest in the Underground Lab, then use the Analyzer to study mutations. The first analysis of a mutation also gives a bonus Copper reward.

If you cannot use the Analyzer yet, continue the Beth questline and revisit the Underground Lab. You have not necessarily missed the Greenhouse unlock.

A simple next-session checklist

  • Check your current Garden level and the next crop milestone.
  • Harvest the crop you are actively progressing on The Garden.
  • Review visitor offers and complete the ones that fit your available materials.
  • At Garden VII, look for the Carpenter and claim the Blueprint from the first completed offer.
  • After unlocking the Greenhouse, check water and adjacent crop effects before planting a full layout.
  • Continue Beth's quest if you want to unlock the Crop Analyzer.

Frequently asked questions

Do normal Garden crops grow while I am offline?

No. The regular Garden crops grow while you are in The Garden. Offline growth is a Greenhouse feature.

What Garden level unlocks the Greenhouse?

Garden Level 7. You also need to complete the Carpenter's first visitor offer and give the resulting Blueprint to Sam.

Do crop milestones count if I harvest somewhere else?

No. Garden crop milestones count when you harvest the crop on The Garden. Harvesting on another island does not advance that Garden milestone.

Is the Crop Analyzer unlocked automatically?

No. It is unlocked through Beth's later quest in the Underground Lab after the Greenhouse progression begins.
