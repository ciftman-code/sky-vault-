How to use Craft Flip

  1. Choose the crafted item and set how many you craft per batch.
  2. Add each recipe ingredient and the quantity required.
  3. Calculate using the latest available Bazaar snapshot.
  4. Check the result against the recipe and current in-game market before crafting.

Prices are indicative averages, not guaranteed fills. A positive estimate can disappear before your orders complete.
