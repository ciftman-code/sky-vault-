⛏️ The mining progression route

Think of mining as three connected jobs: unlock the systems, invest in the tree that supports your activity, then improve the gear that lets you gather resources efficiently. The exact best setup changes with balance updates and your current profile.

  1. Unlock the Dwarven Mines. Follow the in-game introduction and learn the main NPCs, commission board and mining areas.
  2. Make commissions part of your routine. They teach the area and help advance Heart of the Mountain. Pick objectives that overlap with what you are already doing where possible.
  3. Build Heart of the Mountain deliberately. Read the perk descriptions and decide whether your next session is for commissions, powder or gemstone mining before spending tokens.
  4. Collect powder and unlock useful perks. Powder upgrades are long-term account progress. Avoid scattering upgrades randomly; focus on the perks that improve the activity you chose.
  5. Move into the Crystal Hollows when ready. Learn the map, structures, routes and gemstone areas. Bring a plan for inventory space and how you will handle interruptions.
  6. Upgrade your tool and supporting gear. Compare the benefit of each upgrade against its cost. A balanced setup is often better than one premium item with weak supporting stats.
Route rule

Don't buy a high-cost mining item just because it is popular. First check its requirement, your Heart of the Mountain progression, and whether your current activity can use its perks.

🧭 Understand the systems

Heart of the Mountain

Your mining progression tree. Perks and nodes change what your setup is good at. Read each node's current in-game description before assigning upgrades.

Commissions

Short objectives that reward mining progression. Group them by area and objective so you spend more time completing tasks and less time travelling.

Powder

A long-term upgrade resource. Your powder goal should match your intended mining style; do not assume every profile needs the same distribution.

Gemstones

Gemstone mining is sensitive to the combination of tool stats, perk choices, route familiarity and market value. Compare raw and crafted forms before selling.

What to track between sessions

  • Heart of the Mountain level and the next unlock you care about.
  • Powder balances and the next perk milestone.
  • Which commissions are efficient for your current area access.
  • Tool requirements and the exact stats an upgrade adds.
  • Inventory, fuel and other running costs that affect a long session.

🚧 Mining mistakes that waste time

  • Buying a tool before checking its unlock or stat requirements.
  • Following an old perk tree screenshot without checking whether nodes or balance have changed.
  • Mixing powder goals with gemstone-profit sessions and expecting both to be optimal at once.
  • Ignoring route familiarity, inventory space and interruptions when comparing hourly estimates.
  • Assuming a market price is guaranteed. Orders, volume and competition affect what you can actually sell.

✅ Before your next mining session

  • Choose one focus: commissions, powder, a collection, or gemstone gathering.
  • Check your next meaningful Heart of the Mountain unlock.
  • Confirm tool and armor requirements in-game.
  • Prepare inventory space and supplies for the session length.
  • Record your starting coins or progress so you can compare like-for-like later.

Frequently asked questions

Should I focus on powder or gemstones first?

It depends on your account and goal. If important Heart of the Mountain perks are still missing, powder and commissions can be useful long-term progress. If your setup is ready for gemstone mining, focus on the route and stats needed for that activity.

Is the most expensive drill always best?

No. Requirements, perks, your mining tree, and the activity you do matter. Compare the full setup rather than the item's price tag.

Are market profits guaranteed?

No. The Bazaar tool shows estimates from public market data. Prices and order depth can change before an order fills.
